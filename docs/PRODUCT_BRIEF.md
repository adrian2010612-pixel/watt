# WHAT IF v2 Product Brief

## Problem
“What if?” content is naturally interesting, but stories alone can create one-time consumption. WHAT IF turns the question into an interactive learning loop.

## Product
Users start with a real event, change one condition, choose a branch, explore consequences, inspect evidence, predict, compare community thinking, complete a quiz, earn XP and share.

## Initial wedge
Sports + History first. Technology + Future next. Society and community creation after the core loop proves retention.

## Differentiator
Alternate timelines + evidence + branching + prediction + learning + progression.

## Retention hypothesis
Daily scenarios, unfinished branches, streaks, recommendations, community prediction and shareable results create reasons to return.

## Monetisation experiment
Free core loop. Potential WHAT IF+ for deeper AI simulations, advanced branches, exclusive content and ad-free use. Test willingness to pay before treating pricing as validated.

## Main risks
Weak return behaviour, AI hallucinations, poor source quality, content breadth, moderation cost, unclear willingness to pay and expensive generation.

## Response
Measure completed timelines, source claims, separate fact from speculation, focus the initial content wedge, control AI behind a service boundary, moderate community content and test monetisation.
